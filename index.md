# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 11, 2026

## Overview

FreestyleVault is a freestyle rap battle app where you can stay up to date with the latest battles, watch highlights, explore MC profiles, and follow competitions.

## Data Collection

FreestyleVault collects minimal data to provide its features:

- **Device identifiers** — Used to deliver push notifications (FCM token, device ID)
- **Email address** — Only if you sign in with Google or Apple (used for account identification)
- **Favorites and preferences** — Your saved MCs, battles, events, competitions, and notification settings

## Data NOT Collected

FreestyleVault does not:

- Track your location
- Use advertising SDKs
- Sell or share your data with third parties
- Collect browsing history or analytics profiles

## Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud) with encryption in transit. Data is only associated with your account and is not shared.

## Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Profile screen in the app. See our [Account Deletion](account-deletion) page for details.

## Third-Party Services

- **Firebase** (Google) — Authentication, push notifications, data storage
- **YouTube** — Video links are opened externally; YouTube's privacy policy applies

## Content

All competition names, logos, and battle data are used for informational purposes only. FreestyleVault is not affiliated with Red Bull, FMS, Urban Roosters, or any freestyle competition organization.

## Changes

We may update this policy if we add new features. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

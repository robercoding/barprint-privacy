# Account Deletion — FreestyleVault

**Last updated:** July 11, 2026

## How to delete your account

You can delete your FreestyleVault account directly from the app:

1. Open **FreestyleVault**
2. Go to **Profile** (bottom tab)
3. Tap **DELETE ACCOUNT** (below the sign-out button)
4. Confirm the deletion in the dialog

Your account will be permanently deleted immediately.

## What data is deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

- **Account credentials** (Google/Apple sign-in link)
- **Favorites** (saved MCs, battles, events, competitions)
- **Push notification tokens** (device registrations)
- **Notification preferences** (reminder settings)

## What data is NOT stored

FreestyleVault does not collect or store:

- Personal messages or conversations
- Location data
- Payment information
- Photos or media

## Retention period

All data is deleted **immediately** upon account deletion. There is no retention period.

## Not signed in?

If you are using FreestyleVault without signing in (anonymous mode), no personal data is associated with your session. You can simply uninstall the app.
